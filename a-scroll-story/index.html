Chapter II

Research At Harvey
Advancing The Frontier
of Legal Intelligence

Our research connects expert-defined legal standards with new approaches to evaluating, training, and improving models and agents.

n 2026, we introduced Harvey Tenet, our first model trained for legal work, achieving frontier-level performance at a fraction of the cost.

We also open-sourced the Legal Agent Benchmark (LAB), used by frontier labs to evaluate new models. LAB launched with 1,200+ long-horizon legal tasks graded against expert rubrics and now covers in-house contracting, M&A diligence, firm knowledge, and case law.
